NeoBuzz Live Plug-ins
=====================

Neo Filter, Neo EQ, Neo Comp, Neo Delay, Neo Verb and Neo Poly as CLAP, VST3 and (macOS) Audio Unit plug-ins. Part of NeoBuzz Live
(github.com/khuzul2/neo-buzz), a fork of ReBuzz.
