How it's measured: a level test renders 4 s of the machine alone, fed noise at a mix's level (effects, peaks near −9 dBFS) or holding A4 (generators): at its defaults, with every parameter at its minimum and at its maximum at once, and, for machines that misbehave that way and effects with a feedback, resonance, gain or Q parameter, each parameter alone at each end. A level that grew over 4 s is rendered for 30 s: an attack settles, a runaway keeps growing (its last 10 s more than 6 dB over the 10 s before).

Pitch conventions of the generators (the offset `notes_write` applies so the written pitch plays): no sound from a note: 11, offset +0: 11, offset +12: 2, offset -13: 1, unpitched: 5.
