Generated by `tools/machine-profiler.py` (docs/specs/mcp-server.md §12.2, docs/specs/machine-safety.md §3.1) from `Machines/Manifests/machine-profile.json`. Do not edit by hand: run the profiler, then `--report`.

126 machines profiled (35 on linux, 91 on windows). 2 diverging, 55 hot, 0 needing a window, 0 with other problems, 11 with other warnings, 58 clean. Crashes or hangs: 0. Swept parameter by parameter: 65 machines; 6 safe ranges. Slow down after their input goes silent: 0. Suggested roles: 415 parameters on 57 machines.

How it's measured: a level test renders 4 s of the machine alone, fed noise at a mix's level (effects, peaks near −9 dBFS) or holding A4 (generators; a drum kit silent on A4 hit on the key that sounds, every second): at its defaults, with every parameter at its minimum and at its maximum at once, and, for machines that misbehave that way and effects with a feedback, resonance, gain or Q parameter, each parameter alone at each end. A level that grew over 4 s is rendered for 30 s: an attack settles, a runaway keeps growing (its last 10 s more than 6 dB over the 10 s before). A loop's or a resonance's hot end that rings more than 24 dB over its level at the default gets a safe range by bisection too.

In depth (mcp-server.md §12.12): a test signal for effects, played by Neo Poly and read on both sides: a click with time to ring out (latency, decay, echoes), a stepped sine sweep (the response) and, for dynamics, level steps of a three-sine chord (the curve, attack and release); each parameter alone at its ends through it, or a held note on generators, names a suggested role. Generators: velocity response, envelope length, a harmonic sieve where YIN hears no pitch, a drum kit's keys (its manifest's note map, else C2 and D2) and triggers where no note sounds. Every machine: its own work time per second of audio over the 20 s after its input stops, against while it plays.

Pitch conventions of the generators (the offset `notes_write` applies so the written pitch plays): no sound from a note: 11, offset +0: 18, offset +12: 2, offset -13: 1, unpitched: 1.

Built from source (mcp-server.md §12.14, U7): FSM Chorus2, FSM Infector, FSM KickXP, FSM PanzerDelay, FSM PhatMan, FSM WahMan, FSM WahManPro, FSM WahPro2, Jeskola Distortion, Jeskola Noise Generator, Jeskola Trilok, Oomek Aggressor 303, Oomek Exciter, Oomek Masterizer ship rebuilt in place of upstream's binaries, which they render as (`tools/native-compare.py`; FSM WahMan, FSM WahManPro, FSM WahPro2 as upstream's binary on a zeroed heap, the start their fix gives), and are profiled as rebuilt; Jeskola Distortion, Jeskola Noise Generator, Jeskola Trilok are x64 builds of upstream's 32-bit binaries, from sources without a licence (shipped by user decision 2026-11-06 for private use); the other natives are upstream's binaries.
